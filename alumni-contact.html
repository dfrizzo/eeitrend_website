About SMAC

The St. Michael Alumni Connection (SMAC) fosters community for all those whose lives have been touched by St. Michael — promoting Catholic education through fellowship and participation in events.

  • All-Class Reunions Ages 21+
  • Baccalaureate Mass Each June
  • School Founded 80+ Years
